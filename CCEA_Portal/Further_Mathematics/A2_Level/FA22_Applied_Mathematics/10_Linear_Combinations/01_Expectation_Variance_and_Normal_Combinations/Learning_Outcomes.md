## FA22-LINCOMB-LO001
**Official Specification Wording:**
> demonstrate understanding of and use the expressions for $E(aX+bY)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY)$, where $X$ and $Y$ are independent random variables

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> Proofs will not be required. Students are expected to know that if independent $X\sim N(\mu_X,\sigma_X^2)$ and $Y\sim N(\mu_Y,\sigma_Y^2)$, then $aX\pm bY\sim N(a\mu_X\pm b\mu_Y,a^2\sigma_X^2+b^2\sigma_Y^2)$.

**Student-Friendly Meaning:**
> Understand and use the expressions for $E(aX+bY)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY)$, where $X$ and $Y$ are independent random variables

> Use the official wording as the syllabus boundary and apply the elaboration guidance exactly. Do not extend the topic beyond the stated examples, inclusions and exclusions unless a question provides extra structure.
