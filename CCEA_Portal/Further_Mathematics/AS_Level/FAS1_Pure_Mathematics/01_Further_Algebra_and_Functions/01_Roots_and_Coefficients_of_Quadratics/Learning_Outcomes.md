## FAS1-FAF-LO001
**Official Specification Wording:**
> demonstrate understanding of and use the relationship between roots and coefficients of quadratic equations

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> The elaboration expects students to work with roots such as $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of a quadratic polynomial and evaluate expressions including $\alpha^2+\beta^2$, $\alpha^3+\beta^3$, $1/\alpha+1/\beta$ and $(2+\alpha)(2+\beta)$.

**Student-Friendly Meaning:**
> Use Vieta-style relationships between the roots and coefficients of a quadratic.
