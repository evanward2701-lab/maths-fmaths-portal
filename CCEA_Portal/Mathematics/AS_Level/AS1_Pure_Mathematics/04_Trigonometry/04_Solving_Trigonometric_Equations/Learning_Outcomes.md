## AS1-TRIG-LO007
**Official Specification Wording:**
> solve simple trigonometric equations in a given interval, including quadratic equations in $\sin$, $\cos$ and $\tan$ and equations involving multiples of the unknown angle

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> Examples include solving $\cos(2x-60^\circ)=0.5$ for $0^\circ<x<360^\circ$, $1+\sin(2x)=0.5$ for $-180^\circ<x<180^\circ$, $2\sin^2x-\cos x=1$ for $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$, and $\tan(3x)=2$ for $0^\circ\leq x\leq180^\circ$.

**Student-Friendly Meaning:**
> Solve simple trigonometric equations in a given interval, including quadratic equations in $\sin$, $\cos$ and $\tan$ and equations involving multiples of the unknown a...

**Required Depth / Boundaries:**
> Stay with simple trigonometric equations in stated intervals, including quadratics in one trig function and multiples of the unknown angle. General inverse-trig domain/range theory is A2.
