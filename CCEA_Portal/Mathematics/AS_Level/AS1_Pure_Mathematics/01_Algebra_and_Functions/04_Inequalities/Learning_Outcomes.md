## AS1-AF-LO009
**Official Specification Wording:**
> solve linear and quadratic inequalities in a single variable and interpret such inequalities graphically, including inequalities with brackets and fractions

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> Includes inequalities reducible to linear and quadratic inequalities, for example $\frac{a}{x}<b$ implies $ax<bx^2$.

**Student-Friendly Meaning:**
> Solve linear and quadratic inequalities in a single variable and interpret such inequalities graphically, including inequalities with brackets and fractions

**Required Depth / Boundaries:**
> Include linear/quadratic inequalities and those reducible to them. Take care with domains and sign changes in fractional inequalities; do not extend into general rational inequality theory beyond the specified forms.
