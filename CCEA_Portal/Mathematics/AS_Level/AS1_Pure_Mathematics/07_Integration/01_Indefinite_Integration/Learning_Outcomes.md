## AS1-INT-LO002
**Official Specification Wording:**
> integrate $x^n$ excluding $n=-1$, and related sums, differences and constant multiples

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> Includes rational values of $n$. Students should be able to find $f(x)$ given $f'(x)$.

**Student-Friendly Meaning:**
> Integrate $x^n$ excluding $n=-1$, and related sums, differences and constant multiples

**Required Depth / Boundaries:**
> Use the power rule for $n\ne -1$, including rational powers. The $\int 1/x\,dx$ result is not AS1 here and appears at A2.
