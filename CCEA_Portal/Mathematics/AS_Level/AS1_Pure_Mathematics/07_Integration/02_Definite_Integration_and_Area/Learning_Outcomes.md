## AS1-INT-LO004
**Official Specification Wording:**
> use a definite integral to find the area defined by a curve and either axis

**Elaboration Guidance:**
> Includes, for example, the area bounded by $y=x(x-2)$ and the lines $x=-1$ and $x=1$. Includes use of definite integration together with areas of trapeziums and triangles, for example finding the area bounded by $y=x^2$ and the line $y=3x$.
