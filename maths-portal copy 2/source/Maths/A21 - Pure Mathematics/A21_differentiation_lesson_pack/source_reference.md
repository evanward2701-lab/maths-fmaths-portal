## Specification Boundary Notes

The lesson is for standard CCEA GCE Mathematics, not Further Mathematics.

Core content included:
- Differentiating \(e^{kx}\), \(\ln(kx)\), \(\sin(kx)\), \(\cos(kx)\), \(	an(kx)\), and related sums, differences and constant multiples.
- Product, quotient and chain rules.
- Differentiating \(\cosec x\), \(\sec x\), and \(\cot x\).
- Differentiating simple functions and relations defined implicitly or parametrically.
- Finding and interpreting the second derivative.
- Connected rates and contextual derivative modelling.
- Partial support for constructing simple differential equations in context.
