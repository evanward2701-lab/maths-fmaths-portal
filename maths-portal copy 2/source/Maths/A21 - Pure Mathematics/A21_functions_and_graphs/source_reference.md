## CCEA Specification Boundary

### Core CCEA A21-AF content covered

- Definition of a function.
- Domain and range.
- Composite functions.
- Inverse functions and their graphs.
- Modulus function, including modulus inequalities.
- Combined graph transformations of `y=f(x)`.

### CCEA A21-AF content excluded from this specific lesson

- Rational expressions, factorising/cancelling and algebraic division.
- Partial fractions.
- Substantial functions-in-modelling work beyond general interpretation.

These exclusions are not syllabus omissions from the whole course; they are excluded because they are not the focus of the supplied Chapter 2 Functions and Graphs evidence.
