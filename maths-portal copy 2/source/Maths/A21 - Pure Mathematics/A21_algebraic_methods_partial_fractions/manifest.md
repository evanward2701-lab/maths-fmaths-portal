## Syllabus Boundary

### Included Core Content

- Simplifying rational expressions by factorising and cancelling.
- Algebraic division of rational expressions.
- Distinct linear factor partial fractions.
- Repeated squared linear factor partial fractions.
- Improper rational functions requiring quotient plus proper remainder.
- Substitution method.
- Comparing coefficients.
- Checking by recombining.

### Excluded or Enrichment Only

- Proof by contradiction from the broader Dr Frost chapter.
- Cubed repeated linear factors.
- Irreducible quadratic denominator templates.
- Further Mathematics generalisations.
- Full integration using partial fractions, except as a forward link to A21-INT-LO005.
- Edexcel-labelled exam references as CCEA exam authority.
