## On-Spec Core Content Covered

- Definitions of secant, cosecant and cotangent.
- Relationship of sec, cosec and cot to sine, cosine and tangent.
- Distinction between reciprocal trig notation and inverse trig notation.
- Graphs, domains, ranges and asymptotes of sec, cosec and cot.
- Inverse trig functions arcsin, arccos and arctan.
- Restricted domains and ranges for inverse trig functions.
- Identities sec²θ = 1 + tan²θ and cosec²θ = 1 + cot²θ.
- Solving equations involving reciprocal trig functions.
- Proofs involving reciprocal trig functions and identities.
