## Lesson-Specific Evidence

### Chapter_12_Vectors_🎲_(Pure_Year_2)_Transcript.md

Status: supporting evidence only.

Used for transferable vector language and warnings, including:

- magnitude as length;
- unit vector as a vector divided by its magnitude;
- vector between two points as second point minus first point;
- scalar multiples as a way of checking parallel vectors.

Boundary note: the transcript is Pure Year 2 / P2 and includes 3D vector material. Since the supplied CCEA standard Mathematics map places vectors under AS1 and specifies 2D vectors, the 3D material was not treated as CCEA AS1 core.

## Limitations

- The available lesson-specific evidence mainly covers a Pure Year 2 / 3D vectors lesson rather than CCEA AS1 2D vectors.
- The CCEA specification map was therefore used as the boundary authority.
- Some examples in the main lesson are boundary-safe constructed examples rather than transcribed CCEA AS1 lesson examples.
- Visual assets are teaching enhancements based on the CCEA AS1 vector outcomes, not exact reproductions of a CCEA AS1 slide deck.

## Final Source Decision

Core lesson content is CCEA AS1 2D vectors only.

The uploaded Pure Year 2 / P2 3D vectors evidence is logged as enrichment or boundary-risk evidence and excluded from the required core.
