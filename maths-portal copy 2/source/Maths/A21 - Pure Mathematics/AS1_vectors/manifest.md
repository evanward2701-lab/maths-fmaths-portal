## Syllabus Boundary

### Core CCEA AS1 content included

- Two-dimensional vectors.
- \(\mathbf{i}\) and \(\mathbf{j}\) unit vectors.
- Column vector notation.
- Magnitude and direction of a vector.
- Conversion between component form and magnitude/direction form.
- Unit vectors.
- Vector addition.
- Multiplication of vectors by scalars.
- Geometrical interpretations of vector addition, including triangle and parallelogram laws.
- Position vectors.
- Distance between two points represented by position vectors.

### Off-spec / enrichment material excluded from the core

- \(\mathbf{k}\) notation.
- Three-dimensional vectors.
- Three-dimensional distance formula.
- Angles between 3D vectors and coordinate axes.
- Dot product.
- Vector equations of lines.
- 3D mechanics vector applications.
