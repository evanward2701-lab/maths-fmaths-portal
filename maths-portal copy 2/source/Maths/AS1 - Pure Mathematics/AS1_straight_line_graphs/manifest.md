## Syllabus Boundary

### Core content included

- Line equations as sets of points satisfying equations.
- Vertical and horizontal lines, including \(x=2\) and \(y=2\).
- Substitution of coordinates into line equations.
- \(x\)-intercepts and \(y\)-intercepts.
- Gradient as \(m=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\).
- Gradient using two coordinates.
- Collinearity using equal gradients.
- Missing-coordinate gradient problems.
- \(y=mx+c\), including gradient and \(y\)-intercept interpretation.
- Standard form \(ax+by+c=0\).
- Point-gradient form \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\).
- Equations of lines through two points.
- Intersections of two straight lines by simultaneous equations.
- Parallel and perpendicular gradient conditions.
- Distance between two points using Pythagoras.
- Triangle area problems in coordinate geometry.
- Linear modelling, including gradient/intercept interpretation and model limitations.
- Interpolation and extrapolation warnings.

### Partial content

AS1-CG-LO002 is only partially covered because the evidence covers length of a line segment, but does not teach the midpoint formula or midpoint examples.

### Excluded from core

- Further Maths dot product material.
- 3D plane equations.
- Matrix/vector extensions beyond AS1 straight-line coordinate geometry.
- Regression/correlation methods.
- Exponential, quadratic or non-linear modelling as required core content.
- Edexcel C1 material as official CCEA evidence.
