## Syllabus Boundary

### Core content included

- Simplifying algebraic fractions by factorising first.
- Cancelling common algebraic factors only.
- Difference of two squares where required for simplification.
- Polynomial long division by a linear expression.
- Missing zero terms in polynomial division.
- Quotient plus remainder form.
- Remainder theorem.
- Factor theorem.
- Fully factorising cubic expressions after finding one factor.
- Finding unknown coefficients from a given factor.

### Excluded or non-core material

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Algebraic division by non-linear factors | Excluded from core because CCEA AS1 boundary is division by a linear expression only. |
| Difference of two cubes formula | Excluded from core; noted as optional enrichment only. |
| MAT/extension questions | Excluded from core. |
| Proof by deduction chapter material | Deferred to separate proof lesson. |
| Cross-board note saying remainder theorem is no longer in spec | Excluded as CCEA boundary guidance because CCEA includes the remainder theorem. |
