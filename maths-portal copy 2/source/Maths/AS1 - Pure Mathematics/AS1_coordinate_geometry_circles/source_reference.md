## Lesson-Specific Evidence Summary

The Chapter 6 Circles evidence supplied the following content:

- chapter overview for circles;
- midpoints and perpendicular bisectors;
- equation of a circle centred at the origin;
- equation of a circle centred at \((a,b)\);
- finding a circle equation from a diameter;
- completing the square to find centre and radius;
- line-circle intersections;
- discriminant use for secant, tangent and no intersection cases;
- tangent-radius perpendicularity;
- tangents through a known point on the circumference;
- tangents with a given gradient;
- chord perpendicular bisectors and centre-finding;
- circumcircle and angle-in-a-semicircle reasoning.

## Learning Outcomes Used

### Core CCEA AS1-CG Learning Outcomes

- AS1-CG-LO001: demonstrate understanding of and use the equation of a straight line, including the forms \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\) and \(ax+by+c=0\)
- AS1-CG-LO002: demonstrate understanding of how to find the mid-point of a line segment
- AS1-CG-LO003: use the gradient conditions for two straight lines to be parallel or perpendicular
- AS1-CG-LO005: demonstrate understanding of and use the coordinate geometry of the circle, including using the equation of a circle in the forms \((x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2\) and \(x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0\)
- AS1-CG-LO006: find the centre and radius of a circle by completing the square
- AS1-CG-LO007: use the standard circle properties: angle in a semicircle is a right angle, perpendicular from centre to a chord bisects the chord and perpendicularity of radius and tangent
- AS1-CG-LO008: find the equation of the tangent to a circle through a given point on the circumference

### Supporting AS1-AF Learning Outcomes

- AS1-AF-LO004: demonstrate understanding of and use the discriminant of a quadratic function, including the condition for real and repeated roots
- AS1-AF-LO005: complete the square in a quadratic function
- AS1-AF-LO006: solve quadratic equations, including quadratic equations in a function of the unknown
- AS1-AF-LO007: solve simultaneous equations in two variables by elimination and by substitution, including one linear and one quadratic equation
