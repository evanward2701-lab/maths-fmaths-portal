Why this works:

The point on the unit circle is \((\cos\theta,\sin\theta)\). So cosine is the left-right movement and sine is the up-down movement.

Tangent warning:

Here \(\cos\theta=0\), so \(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\) is undefined. This happens at \(90^\circ\) and \(270^\circ\), plus coterminal angles.
