## Syllabus Boundary Summary

### Core CCEA AS1 Content Included

- Definitions of \(\sin\theta\), \(\cos\theta\), \(\tan\theta\).
- Unit-circle interpretation for all arguments.
- Exact trig values for \(30^\circ\), \(45^\circ\), \(60^\circ\).
- Symmetry and periodicity of sine, cosine and tangent.
- \(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\).
- \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\).
- Identity proofs.
- Simplifying trigonometric expressions.
- Simple trigonometric equations in stated intervals.
- Equations involving multiples of the unknown angle.
- Quadratic equations in \(\sin\), \(\cos\) and \(\tan\).
