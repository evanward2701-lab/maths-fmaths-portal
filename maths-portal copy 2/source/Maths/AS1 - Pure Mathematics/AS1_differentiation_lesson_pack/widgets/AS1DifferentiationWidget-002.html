Secant to Tangent Explorer

For \(y=x^2\), the exact gradient at \(x=5\) is \(2x=10\). Move \(h\) towards \(0\) and watch the secant gradient approach \(10\).

Fixed point: (5, 25)
h = 1.00
Nearby point = (6.00, 36.00)

When h = 1, the secant gradient is 11. Move h smaller to improve the approximation.
