The volume function is V = 18x − (2/3)x³. Move the slider to see how the volume changes. The maximum occurs where dV/dx = 0.

x = 3.00 m
Volume: V = 18x − (2/3)x³
Derivative: dV/dx = 18 − 2x²
Second derivative: d²V/dx² = −4x
V = 36.00 m³
dV/dx = 0.00
d²V/dx² = -12.00

At x = 3, dV/dx = 0 and d²V/dx² < 0, so this is a maximum.
