## Cross-Board and Extension Material

The DrFrost evidence includes references to Edexcel, MAT, STEP and Pearson materials.

Decision:

- Cross-board examples were used only where the mathematics directly matched CCEA AS1 Algebra and Functions outcomes.
- Cross-board material was not treated as CCEA authority.
- Off-spec extension content was logged and excluded from the required core lesson.

## Off-Spec or Boundary-Risk Content

| Evidence item | Why risky | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Quartic polynomial sketches | CCEA AS1 polynomial sketching boundary is degree `x<=3` | Excluded from core; optional enrichment only |
| Quintic and higher polynomial sketches | Beyond supplied AS1 boundary | Excluded from core |
| Formal “point of inflection” vocabulary | Evidence notes this is not required in the newer A Level syllabus | Used descriptively only |
| STEP/MAT/Edexcel extension prompts | Cross-board | Used only when matching CCEA AS1 skills, otherwise enrichment |
| Pearson textbook exercises not fully supplied | Full pages not available | Not reproduced beyond visible slide evidence |

## Limitations

- Screenshots PDF was visual-only, so it was used cautiously.
- Some slide content references textbook exercises, but the full textbook extract was not supplied.
- Quartic examples were present in evidence but excluded from the CCEA AS1 core lesson boundary.
- SVG and widget files have been written, but browser/LaTeX rendering has not been manually inspected beyond file creation.

## Final Source Decision

The lesson pack is specification-controlled, evidence-backed and boundary-logged. Core content remains within AS1 Algebra and Functions, with off-spec polynomial extension material separated from required CCEA content.
