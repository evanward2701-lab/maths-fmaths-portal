## Core Content Included

The lesson covers:

- $y=a^x$, with $a>0$;
- exponential growth for $a>1$;
- exponential decay for $0<a<1$;
- $y=e^x$ and simple transformations;
- logarithms as inverses of exponentials;
- $\ln x$ as the inverse of $e^x$;
- graph features of logarithmic functions;
- laws of logarithms and proofs;
- exponential equations;
- exponential inequalities;
- growth and decay modelling;
- continuous compound interest, population growth, radioactive decay and drug concentration decay as required modelling contexts;
- model interpretation and long-term reasonableness.
