The lesson pack used the project evidence hierarchy:

1. CCEA Mathematics specification map.
2. Project module map and evidence checklist.
3. Lesson-specific PDF evidence.
4. Teacher transcript.
5. Screenshot evidence.
6. Cross-board enrichment only where aligned with CCEA boundaries.

If evidence conflicted with the CCEA specification boundary, the CCEA boundary was treated as authoritative.

## Cross-Board and Third-Party Notes

The lesson-specific content comes from DrFrostMaths/Pearson/Edexcel-style Year 1 Pure Mathematics evidence. This material was treated as cross-board support, not as an independent authority. Only content matching the CCEA AS1 Integration boundary was treated as core.

| Evidence item | Why excluded |
|---|---|
| \(\int \frac1x\,dx\) as an integration result | Not part of AS1 power-rule integration. The AS1 boundary excludes \(n=-1\). |
| Surface areas and volumes | Mentioned in evidence overview but not in CCEA AS1 Integration. |
