## Learning Outcome IDs

### Core coverage

- AS1-AF-LO001: demonstrate understanding of and use the laws of indices for all rational exponents
- AS1-AF-LO002: use and manipulate surds, including rationalising the denominator
- AS1-AF-LO010: manipulate polynomials algebraically, including expanding brackets and collecting like terms, factorisation and simple algebraic division

### Partial / boundary coverage

- AS1-AF-LO011: use the remainder and factor theorems

This lesson includes simple cubic factorisation through common factors and related factorisation techniques, but it does not teach the factor theorem or remainder theorem.
