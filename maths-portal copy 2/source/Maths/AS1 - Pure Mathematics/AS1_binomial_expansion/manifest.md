## Syllabus Boundary Summary

Core CCEA AS1 content included:

- Binomial expansion of $(a+bx)^n$ for positive integer $n$.
- Pascal’s triangle for small positive integer powers.
- Factorial notation $n!$.
- Choose notation ${}^nC_r$ and $\binom nr$.
- General term $\binom nr a^{n-r}b^r$.
- Single-term and coefficient extraction.
- First few terms in ascending powers of $x$.
- Estimation using truncated expansions where appropriate.

Excluded or enrichment-only material:

- Fractional and negative binomial powers.
- Generalised binomial theorem.
- Pascal’s Rule as required examinable proof.
- STEP, MAT and AEA extension material as required core.
- Cross-board exam labels as CCEA-specific evidence.
