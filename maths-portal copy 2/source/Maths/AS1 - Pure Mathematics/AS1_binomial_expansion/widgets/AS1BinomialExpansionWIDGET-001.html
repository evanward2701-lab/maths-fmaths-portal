AS1 Binomial Expansion Builder

Build terms of (a + bx)n using Pascal coefficients and nCr.

AS1 boundary: this widget uses the finite binomial expansion for positive integer powers only. Fractional and negative powers are Year 2 territory, so the widget politely shuts that gate.
