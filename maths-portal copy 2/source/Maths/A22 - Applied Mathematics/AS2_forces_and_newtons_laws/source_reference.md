## Core Mathematical Content Preserved

- Force as a vector.
- Resolving forces into perpendicular components.
- $F\cos\theta$ for the component adjacent to $\theta$.
- $F\sin\theta$ for the component opposite to $\theta$.
- Importance of redrawing a resolved force diagram.
- Equilibrium as zero resultant force.
- Forces right equal forces left.
- Forces up equal forces down.
- Newton's second law as resultant force $=ma$.
- Weight $W=mg$.
- Weight acts vertically downward.
- Normal reaction acts perpendicular to the surface.
- Smooth surface means no friction.
- Rough surface means friction must be considered.
- Friction model $F\leq\mu R$.
- Limiting friction $F=\mu R$.
- Inclined plane components $mg\sin\theta$ and $mg\cos\theta$.
- Light inextensible strings.
- Smooth pulleys and common tension.
- Separate strings requiring separate tensions.
- Exam warnings about diagrams, signs, directions and units.
