## Lesson-Specific Evidence Sources

### MechYr2-Chp4-Moments.pdf

Used for:

- door-handle motivation;
- rigid bodies overview;
- definition of moment;
- force times perpendicular distance;
- clockwise and anticlockwise moments;
- resultant moments;
- rigid body equilibrium;
- rods on supports;
- uniform rods;
- non-uniform rods;
- centres of mass;
- tilting.

Core extracted ideas:

- increasing distance from the point of rotation increases turning effect;
- a rigid body takes size into account;
- moments measure turning effect;
- distance must be perpendicular to the force;
- in equilibrium, resultant force is zero;
- in equilibrium, resultant moment about any point is zero;
- on the point of tilting, reaction at another support is zero.

### Chapter_47_Rigid_Bodies Transcript

Used for:

- teacher explanation of why distance must be perpendicular;
- distinction between particles and rigid bodies;
- statement that rigid bodies can rotate;
- moment as force times perpendicular distance;
- equilibrium as zero resultant force and zero resultant moment;
- method advice: only two tools are resolving and taking moments;
- advice to choose the point that removes awkward unknowns;
- worked examples on rods, supports, suspended beams and tensions;
- explanation of uniform and non-uniform rods;
- explanation of tilting and zero reaction;
- ladder and hinge method commentary.

Core extracted ideas:

- the distance in moments is the perpendicular distance;
- a rigid body has size and can rotate;
- in equilibrium, clockwise moments equal anticlockwise moments;
- students may take moments about any point;
- choosing a moment point can eliminate unknown reactions or tensions;
- draw the diagram carefully before forming equations.
