## Specification Boundary

Core content:
- Projectile motion under gravity in two dimensions.
- Horizontal acceleration \(a_x=0\).
- Vertical acceleration \(a_y=-g\), assuming upwards is positive.
- Resolution of initial velocity into \(U\cos\theta\) and \(U\sin\theta\).
- Time of flight, range, greatest height and trajectory equation.
- Vector projectile motion using \(\mathbf{i}\) and \(\mathbf{j}\).
- Modelling assumptions: particle model, constant gravity, no air resistance unless stated otherwise.

Excluded or enrichment-only content:
- Arc length of projectile path.
- MAT/UKMT extension material.
- Maximum range proof treated only as optional enrichment unless explicitly required by a question.
- Cross-board branding, mark schemes or exam-board-specific wording not treated as CCEA authority.
