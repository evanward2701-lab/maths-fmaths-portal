## Core Authority

### CCEA GCE Mathematics Specification Map

Used as the authority for:
- Unit identity.
- Topic code.
- Learning outcome IDs.
- Official syllabus boundary.
- A22 Kinematics scope.

Relevant topic:
- Unit: A22
- Topic: Kinematics
- LO IDs: A22-KIN-LO001, A22-KIN-LO002, A22-KIN-LO003, A22-KIN-LO004

The specification map identifies A22 Kinematics as having four learning outcomes, A22-KIN-LO001 to A22-KIN-LO004. It also states that A22-KIN-LO004 is “solve problems involving projectiles” and that derivation of formulae for time of flight, range, greatest height and the equation of the path of flight may be required.
