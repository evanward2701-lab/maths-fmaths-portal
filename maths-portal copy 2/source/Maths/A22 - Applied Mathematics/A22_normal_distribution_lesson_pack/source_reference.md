## Official Specification Alignment

The CCEA specification map identifies A22 Statistical distributions as `A22-NORMAL`, with learning outcomes `A22-NORMAL-LO001` to `A22-NORMAL-LO003`. It also identifies A22 Statistical hypothesis testing as `A22-HT`, with `A22-HT-LO001` to `A22-HT-LO005`. The normal-distribution lesson uses the three `A22-NORMAL` outcomes as core and includes normal-mean hypothesis testing as adjacent support under `A22-HT-LO004`.
