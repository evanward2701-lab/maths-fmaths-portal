# AS2DataPresentationMERMAID-010

**Asset ID:** AS2DataPresentationMERMAID-010  
**Source:** Comparing box plots and distributions evidence  
**Related lesson section:** Exam Technique, Comparing Distributions  
**Purpose:** Give students a quick decision flow for two-mark comparison questions.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Compare two distributions] --> B[Choose one measure of location]
    A --> C[Choose one measure of spread]
    B --> B1[Usually median]
    B1 --> B2[Write which is greater or lower]
    B2 --> B3[Interpret in context]
    C --> C1[Usually IQR or range]
    C1 --> C2[Write which is greater or lower]
    C2 --> C3[Interpret in context]
    B3 --> D[Complete comparison]
    C3 --> D
    D --> E[Avoid bare statements with no context]
```
