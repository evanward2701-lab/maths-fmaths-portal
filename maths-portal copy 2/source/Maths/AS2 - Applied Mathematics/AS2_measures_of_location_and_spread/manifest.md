## CCEA Alignment Summary

The main lesson is anchored in AS2-DPI. AS2-DPI-LO002 covers interpretation of measures of central tendency and variation, including standard deviation and variance. AS2-DPI-LO003 covers calculating standard deviation and variance from summary statistics, and its elaboration includes grouped-data median estimation, appropriate midpoints, and use of either sigma_n or sigma_{n-1} as appropriate.
