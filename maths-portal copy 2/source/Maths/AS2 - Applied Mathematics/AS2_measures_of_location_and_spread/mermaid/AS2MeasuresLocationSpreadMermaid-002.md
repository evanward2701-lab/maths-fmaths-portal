## Purpose
Clarify that `x` in statistics can represent a collection of values, while `x-bar` is one single summary value.

```mermaid

    A["Statistical variable \\(x\\)"] --> B["Represents a collection of values"]

    B --> D["Can be added as a collection"]
    D --> E["\\(\\sum x\\)<br/>sum of all \\(x\\)-values"]
    B --> F["Can be summarised by one value"]
    F --> G["\\(\\bar{x}\\)<br/>mean of the \\(x\\)-values"]
    H["Algebraic variable \\(x\\)"] --> I["Usually represents one value"]
```
