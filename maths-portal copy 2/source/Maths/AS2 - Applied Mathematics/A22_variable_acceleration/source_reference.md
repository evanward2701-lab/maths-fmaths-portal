Relevant specification point:

- A22-KIN-LO001: use calculus in kinematics for motion in a straight line:

  \[
  v=\frac{ds}{dt},
  \qquad
  a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2s}{dt^2},
  \qquad
  s=\int v\,dt,
  \qquad
  v=\int a\,dt.
  \]

The elaboration states that displacement, velocity and acceleration will be given as functions of time.
