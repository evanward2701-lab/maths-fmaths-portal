## Cross-Board Notes

This lesson uses cross-board or third-party evidence only where it matches the CCEA AS2 Kinematics boundary.

Core CCEA alignment remains:
- Kinematics language.
- Displacement-time and velocity-time graphs.
- Constant acceleration formulae in a straight line.
- Vertical motion under gravity as supporting mechanics content.

## Final Source Boundary

The lesson pack should not be treated as full AS2 Kinematics coverage because AS2-KIN-LO004 is not fully covered. It is a focused Constant Acceleration lesson covering straight-line motion, graph methods, SUVAT and vertical motion under gravity.
