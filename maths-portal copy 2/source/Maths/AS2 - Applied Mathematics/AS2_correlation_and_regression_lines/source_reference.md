## Evidence Used as Core Lesson Support

The following content from the lesson evidence was used because it matches CCEA AS2-DPI boundaries:

- Bivariate data
- Scatter diagrams
- Positive, negative, strong, weak and no correlation
- Informal interpretation of correlation
- Independent/explanatory and dependent/response variables
- Regression-line interpretation when the equation is given
- Interpreting gradient and intercept in context
- Suitability of a linear regression model
- Interpolation and extrapolation
- Correlation does not imply causation
