## On-Spec Core Content Covered

- Population and sample.
- Sampling unit and sampling frame.
- Census.
- Census vs sample advantages and disadvantages.
- Destructive testing.
- Informal inference from a sample.
- Simple random sampling.
- Stratified sampling.
- Proportional allocation in stratified sampling.
- Choosing and critiquing sampling techniques.
- Understanding that different samples can lead to different conclusions.
- Qualitative, quantitative, discrete and continuous data.

---

## Supplementary Content Status

Systematic, quota and opportunity sampling were included to support method choice and critique because the evidence repeatedly uses them. They are not treated as higher-priority than the CCEA-named simple random and stratified sampling methods.
