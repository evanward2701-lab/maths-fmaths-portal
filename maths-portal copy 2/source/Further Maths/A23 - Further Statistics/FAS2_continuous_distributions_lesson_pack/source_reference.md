## Final evidence boundary statement

This pack teaches CCEA `FAS2-DIST` continuous distributions as part of Further AS 2 Applied Mathematics, Section C: Statistics. Core content is limited to p.d.f.s, interval probabilities, CDFs, mean, variance, standard deviation, transformation rules and simple continuous distribution models. Cross-board evidence is filtered through the CCEA boundary. Ordinary A-Level Mathematics evidence is bridge context only.
