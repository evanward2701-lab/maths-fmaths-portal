| Content found | Why excluded from core |
|---|---|
| t-based confidence intervals for one mean | Official `FA22-TDIST` LOs in the supplied boundary focus on hypothesis tests and when to use t, not confidence intervals |
| t-based confidence intervals for difference of means | Treated as FA22 estimation enrichment unless a separate CCEA LO is supplied |
| Non-parametric tests such as Wilcoxon and Mann-Whitney | Not part of supplied CCEA `FA22-TDIST` boundary |
| ANOVA | Not part of supplied CCEA `FA22-TDIST` boundary |
| Fixed threshold “small means n < 30” | Useful heuristic, but not official CCEA wording in the supplied boundary |
| Dr Frost non-pooled two-sample formula | CCEA route for this topic is treated as pooled variance where equal unknown variance is assumed |
