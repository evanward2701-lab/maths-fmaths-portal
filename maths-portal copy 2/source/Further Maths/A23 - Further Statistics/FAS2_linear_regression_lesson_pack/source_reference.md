## Missing Evidence and Limitations

- Full OCR text from the screenshot PDF was not available.
- Some diagram details were visible only in preview, so no uninspected visual detail is claimed.
- The spring example has a numerical discrepancy between transcribed totals and stated `Sxy`.
- The carbon steel example appears to contain an OCR/speech ambiguity in the final temperature.
- The RSS and `r` example may contain a missing digit in RSS.
- The official CCEA formula booklet extract was referenced by evidence but not directly supplied.

## Final Evidence Boundary Statement

The lesson is controlled by the CCEA FAS2-BIV boundary. Core content is limited to explanatory and response variables, PMCC context, least squares regression lines, prediction within the explanatory-variable range and dangers of extrapolation. Residuals, coding and RSS are included as evidence-backed supporting content around least squares regression. Cross-board material is excluded unless it supports confirmed CCEA-relevant content.
