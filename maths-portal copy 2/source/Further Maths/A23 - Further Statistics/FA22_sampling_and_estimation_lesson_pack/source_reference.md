Ordinary A-Level Maths sources were used only as bridge context for AS2 sampling, AS2 data presentation and variance, A22 normal distribution and A22 hypothesis-testing language. They were not used as Further Mathematics syllabus authority.

## Cross-board source notes

The Dr Frost S3 PDF is cross-board support. It was used only where CCEA FA22-EST confirms the content is on-spec: statistics and sampling distributions, estimator and bias language, unbiased estimator of variance, standard error, CLT and confidence intervals for a population mean.
