F-test for Comparing Two Variances

Off-spec enrichment widget for comparing two independent normal population variances.

Boundary note: this is enrichment, not confirmed CCEA core. The order of the variance ratio controls the order of the degrees of freedom.
Under H₀: σ₁² = σ₂², use F = S₁² / S₂² with F ~ Fn₁−1,n₂−1.
Enter values, then calculate. For lower-tail work, you may enter a lower critical value or use the reciprocal rule externally.
