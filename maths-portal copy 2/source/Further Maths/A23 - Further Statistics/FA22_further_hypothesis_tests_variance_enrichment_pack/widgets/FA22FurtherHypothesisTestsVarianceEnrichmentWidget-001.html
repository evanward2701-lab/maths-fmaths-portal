One-Variance χ² Test Builder

Off-spec enrichment widget for testing a claim about a population variance.

Boundary note: this is enrichment, not confirmed CCEA core. The CCEA-safe overlap is calculating S² as an unbiased estimator of σ².
Test statistic: T = (n − 1)S² / σ₀², with T ~ χ²n−1 under H₀.
Enter values, then calculate. You provide table critical values so the widget stays exam-table friendly.
