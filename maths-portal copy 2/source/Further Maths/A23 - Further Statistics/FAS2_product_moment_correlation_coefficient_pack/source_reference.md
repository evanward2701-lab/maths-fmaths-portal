## Final evidence boundary statement

This lesson pack teaches CCEA FAS2 `FAS2-BIV-LO001`: calculate the product-moment correlation coefficient and understand its use, interpretation and limitations. Ordinary A-Level Maths sources are used only to bridge prior knowledge. Cross-board sources support PMCC only where aligned with the CCEA Further Mathematics boundary. Spearman’s rank and related non-parametric/hypothesis-testing material are preserved in the source reference but excluded from core lesson content.
