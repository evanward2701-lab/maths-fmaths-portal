# Source Reference - FA21 Maclaurin Series Lesson Pack

## Topic Boundary Statement

This lesson pack is for CCEA GCE Further Mathematics, `FA21` Further A2 1 Pure Mathematics, topic code `FA21-FAF`, with a Maclaurin series focus.

The core CCEA lesson content is restricted to:

- `FA21-FAF-LO004`: find the Maclaurin series of a function, including the general term;
- `FA21-FAF-LO005`: recognise and use the Maclaurin series for `e^x`, `ln(1+x)`, `sin x`, `cos x` and `(1+x)^n`, and be aware of the range of values of `x` for which they are valid;
- `FA21-FAF-LO006`: derive the series expansions of simple compound functions;
- `FA21-FAF-LO007`: demonstrate understanding of and use the standard small-angle approximations `sin x ≈ x`, `cos x ≈ 1 - x^2/2` and `tan x ≈ x`, where `x` is in radians.

## Cross-Board Source Notes

The uploaded DrFrost/Pearson lesson-specific material is headed as Further Pure 1 Chapter 6: Taylor Series. It contains:

- Taylor expansions about arbitrary `x = a`;
- limits using Taylor/Maclaurin expansions;
- indeterminate forms including `0/0`, `∞/∞` and `0 × ∞`;
- differential-equation series solutions;
- Edexcel/Pearson exam comments.

These elements are mathematically useful but not treated as CCEA core unless supported by the `FA21-FAF` learning outcomes. In this lesson pack, they are logged as boundary-risk, optional enrichment or excluded from core teaching.
