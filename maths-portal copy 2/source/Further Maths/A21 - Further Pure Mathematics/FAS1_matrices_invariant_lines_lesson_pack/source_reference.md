## Off-Spec Content Excluded

Eigenvalue/eigenvector methods were not taught as the CCEA method for invariant lines. The lesson uses the CCEA-safe transformation method: set `y = mx`, transform a general point `[x, mx]^T`, then impose `y' = mx'`.
