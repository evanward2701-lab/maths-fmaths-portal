Induction Proof Skeleton Builder

Build the structure of a CCEA-style proof by induction for divisibility statements. Each line is exact proof language, not calculator pattern-checking.

CCEA-core route: For this lesson, induction is the official proof method. Divisor notation is support language: d | E means E = dt for some integer t.
  • ✓ Define P(n).
  • ✓ Prove the base case.
  • ✓ Assume P(k), not P(k+1).
  • ✓ Translate divisibility into E_k = dt, where t ∈ ℤ.
  • ✓ Prove the expression for k+1 is d × integer.
  • ✓ Finish with “therefore by mathematical induction”.
