## Cross-Board / Non-CCEA Notes

The uploaded material is labelled FP2 Number Theory. `FP2` is not one of the permitted CCEA Further Mathematics unit prefixes in this project. Therefore the uploaded material was not treated as official CCEA topic authority. It was used only as language support or optional enrichment where it did not conflict with the CCEA boundary.
