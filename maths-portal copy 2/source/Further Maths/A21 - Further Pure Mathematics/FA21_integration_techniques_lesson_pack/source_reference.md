## Ordinary A-Level Maths Bridge Sources

Ordinary A-Level Mathematics sources were used only to explain prior knowledge: integration by parts, definite integration, substitution/reverse chain rule, trigonometric identities, exact trig values and recurrence-style notation. They were not used as Further Mathematics authority.

## Cross-Board Source Notes

No cross-board source was deliberately used. The teacher transcript and FP2-style evidence were used only where the supplied CCEA Further Mathematics map supported the content.

## Missing Evidence

- CCEA extract explicitly confirming arc length and surface area as core content.
- Original textbook pages separate from screenshots.
- Original examination papers and mark schemes for the exam-style examples.
- Full parsed text from the 150-page screenshot PDF.

## Visual Evidence Limitations

The screenshot PDF contained images only for this workflow. Because no text could be parsed, the lesson avoids claiming uninspected diagram details. Visual assets were created as teaching enhancements from the on-spec reduction formula evidence.

## Off-Spec Content Excluded

- Arc length of Cartesian, parametric or polar curves.
- Surface area of revolution.
- Polar arc length or polar surface-style results.

These appeared in the uploaded chapter evidence or surrounding FP2-style topic context, but were not confirmed by the supplied CCEA Further Mathematics boundary for this lesson.

## Final Evidence Boundary Statement

The core lesson is restricted to CCEA-supported FA21 Further Calculus content: repeated integration by parts and simple reduction formulae in integration, with hyperbolic examples used only as supporting synoptic material where compatible with the supplied CCEA map.
