## Off-spec content excluded

The following content appeared in the lesson-specific FP2 evidence but was excluded from the CCEA FAS1 core lesson:

- transformations from the `z`-plane to the `w`-plane;
- `w=z+a+ib`, `w=kz`, `w=iz` transformations;
- Möbius transformations;
- ratio-of-distance loci such as `|z-z1|=k|z-z2|`;
- quotient-argument circular arc loci such as `arg((z-z1)/(z-z2))=theta`;
- cross-board FP2 exam-question material not confirmed as CCEA FAS1 core.

## Final evidence boundary statement

This lesson pack is controlled by the CCEA Further Mathematics `FAS1-CN` boundary. The core lesson teaches simple Argand loci and regions only:

- `|z-a|=r`;
- `|z-a|=|z-b|`;
- `arg(z-a)=theta`;
- simple modulus and argument regions.

Advanced FP2 evidence is logged, but not taught as core.
