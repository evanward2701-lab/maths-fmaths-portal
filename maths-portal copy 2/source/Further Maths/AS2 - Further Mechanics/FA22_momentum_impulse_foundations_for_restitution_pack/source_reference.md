## Final Evidence Boundary Statement

This pack is a bridge-prerequisite lesson for `FA22-REST`. It teaches momentum, impulse, PCLM and vector impulse as foundations for direct collision modelling. It does not complete the CCEA FA22 Restitution topic because the current evidence does not provide Newton’s law of restitution or full CCEA-style restitution examples.
