## Official CCEA boundary statement

This lesson is controlled by CCEA Further Mathematics topic `FA22-REST`, Restitution:

- FA22-REST-LO001: demonstrate understanding of and use Newton's law of restitution.
- FA22-REST-LO002: solve problems involving direct elastic collisions between smooth spheres or between a smooth sphere and a fixed plane.

The CCEA boundary excludes questions involving impulsive tensions in strings.
