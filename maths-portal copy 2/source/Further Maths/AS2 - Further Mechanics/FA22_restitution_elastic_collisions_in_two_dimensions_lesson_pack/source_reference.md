## CCEA boundary statement

This is a CCEA GCE Further Mathematics `FA22-REST` lesson. The controlling learning outcomes are:

```text
FA22-REST-LO001
FA22-REST-LO002
```

Core content is Newton’s law of restitution and direct elastic collisions between smooth spheres or between a smooth sphere and a fixed plane.

## Ordinary A-Level Maths bridge sources

Ordinary A-Level Mathematics material is used only to explain prerequisites and bridges:

- impulse and momentum;
- coefficient of restitution in one dimension;
- velocity components;
- speed as magnitude;
- trigonometric resolution;
- vector and scalar product basics.

Ordinary A-Level sources do not override the Further Mathematics specification.
