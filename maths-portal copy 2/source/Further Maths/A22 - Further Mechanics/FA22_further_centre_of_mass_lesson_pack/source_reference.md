## Ordinary A-Level Maths Bridge Sources

Ordinary A-Level Mathematics sources are used only to explain prior knowledge: integration, areas, area between curves, volumes of revolution, moments, friction, limiting equilibrium and trigonometry. They are not used as Further Mathematics authority.

## Cross-Board Source Notes

The supplied lesson PDF is Pearson/Edexcel-style support. It is used only where it matches the CCEA `FA22-FCOM` syllabus boundary. Edexcel wording is not used as syllabus authority.

## Optional Enrichment Not Required by CCEA

Polar sector and arc derivations are included as enrichment because they appear in supplied lesson evidence. They are not treated as core CCEA proof requirements unless a question explicitly asks for that derivation.

## Final Evidence Boundary Statement

The CCEA `FA22-FCOM` specification boundary controls the core lesson. Ordinary A-Level Mathematics material is bridge context only. Cross-board/Pearson/Edexcel material is included only where it supports on-spec CCEA Further Mathematics content and is not used as syllabus authority.
