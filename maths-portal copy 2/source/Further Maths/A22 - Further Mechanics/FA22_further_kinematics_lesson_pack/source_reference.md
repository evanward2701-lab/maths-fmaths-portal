## Final evidence boundary statement

This lesson pack is syllabus-led by the CCEA GCE Further Mathematics topic `FA22-FKIN`. The supplied lesson-specific evidence strongly supports `FA22-FKIN-LO002` on straight-line variable acceleration with acceleration as a function of time, displacement or velocity. The supplied evidence is weak for `FA22-FKIN-LO001` on three-dimensional kinematics using calculus and \(\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}\), so that strand is included because CCEA requires it, with the limitation explicitly logged.
