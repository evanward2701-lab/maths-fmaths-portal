Closed Form Verifier

Asset ID: FAS2RecurrenceRelationshipsWidget-002

Source: AI-proposed teaching enhancement based on the recurrence lesson evidence.

Purpose: Check a closed form against a recurrence by substituting shifted terms such as u_(n-1) or by checking characteristic-root conditions.

Verification mode

This widget uses structured templates rather than arbitrary expression parsing. That keeps the algebra transparent and avoids calculator goblins hiding in the syntax.

Linear closed form inputs

Closed form: u_n = pn + q. Recurrence: u_n = u_(n-1) + k.

Affine exponential inputs

Closed form: u_n = A a^n + L. Recurrence: u_n = a u_(n-1) + c.

Second-order root-check inputs

Closed form: u_n = A alpha^n + B beta^n. Recurrence: u_n = s u_(n-1) + t u_(n-2).
