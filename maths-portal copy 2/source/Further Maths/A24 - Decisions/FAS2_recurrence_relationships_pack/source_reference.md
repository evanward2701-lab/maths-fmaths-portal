## Cross-board source notes

The uploaded transcript uses FP2/D2 recurrence-relations terminology. It is used only where it matches the CCEA `FAS2-REC` boundary. The transcript states that proof by induction belongs to FP2 rather than D2 in that source. Proof by induction is excluded from this FAS2 recurrence lesson.

The transcript labels some second-order recurrence work as A2 in its own context. CCEA `FAS2-REC-LO002` includes Fibonacci-type relations, so Fibonacci-type second-order recurrence content is included.
