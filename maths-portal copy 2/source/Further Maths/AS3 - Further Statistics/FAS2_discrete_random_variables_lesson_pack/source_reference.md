## Ordinary A-Level Maths Bridge Sources

Ordinary A-Level Maths sources were used only to explain background knowledge: frequency table mean, probability totals, variance and standard deviation, coding of data, and random variable notation. They do not override the CCEA Further Mathematics specification.

## Cross-Board Source Notes

The uploaded lesson evidence includes DrFrostMaths, Edexcel S1 old references and Pearson exercise references. These are not CCEA authority. They are used only where the CCEA Further Mathematics specification confirms the same mathematics is on-spec for discrete probability distributions, expected value, variance and linear transformations.

Cross-board examples are not labelled as CCEA past-paper questions.
