Binomial coefficient targeter

Bridge from ordinary binomial expansion to Further Maths coefficient extraction.

CCEA core lane: coefficient extraction

Input expression

Find [t^r](a+bt)^n

Output

Enter a, b, n, and r.

Method reminder

[t^r](a+bt)^n = C(n,r)a^(n-r)b^r

This works because the r-th binomial term contains (bt)^r=b^r t^r.
