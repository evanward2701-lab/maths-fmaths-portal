Coefficient extraction checker

Core FA22 generating-functions practice: enter coefficients by power, then ask for [t^r]G(t).

CCEA core lane: generating functions

Input

Example: 6,-4,9,0,0,3 represents 6-4t+9t²+3t⁵.

Output

Enter coefficients and choose a power.

Method reminder

[t^r]G(t) means “the coefficient of t^r in G(t)”.

If a power is missing, its coefficient is 0.
