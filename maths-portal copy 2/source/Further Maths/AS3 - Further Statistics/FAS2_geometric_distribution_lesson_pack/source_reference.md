## Ordinary A-Level Maths bridge sources

Ordinary A-Level Mathematics sources were used only to explain prior knowledge: probability, binomial distribution, discrete random variables, expectation, variance and geometric series. They do not override the CCEA Further Mathematics specification boundary.

## Cross-board source notes

Dr Frost / Pearson Further Statistics 1 evidence was used only where the supplied CCEA FAS2-DIST specification map confirms the content is on-spec. Cross-board evidence does not expand the CCEA core.

## Off-spec content excluded

- Negative binomial distribution.
- Third success on the fifteenth attempt.
- Bernoulli, beta, multinomial, multivariate and Dirichlet distribution material.
- Coupon collector’s problem as core content.
- Probability generating functions.

## Final evidence boundary statement

The CCEA Further Mathematics specification map governs the lesson boundary. The core lesson is geometric distribution under FAS2-DIST. Negative binomial appears in the supplied FS1 evidence but is excluded from core because it is not in the supplied CCEA FAS2-DIST LO list.
