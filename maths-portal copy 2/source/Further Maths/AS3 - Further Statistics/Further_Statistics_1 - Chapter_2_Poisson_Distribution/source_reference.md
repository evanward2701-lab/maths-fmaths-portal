The CCEA Further Mathematics specification map controls the syllabus boundary. The lesson-specific Dr Frost/Pearson-style FS1 evidence was used only where the content matches the CCEA FAS2-DIST boundary.

Ordinary A-Level Mathematics sources were used only as bridge context for:

- mean and variance.

Ordinary Mathematics sources do not override CCEA Further Mathematics boundaries.

This lesson pack is a CCEA FAS2 Statistics lesson on Poisson Distribution. It covers the Poisson model, probability calculations, rate scaling, modelling assumptions, mean and variance, and bridge context from ordinary A-Level Mathematics. Cross-board material is excluded from core unless it directly supports the confirmed CCEA FAS2-DIST boundary.
